About Us

Good coffee, good mood.

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café is a cosy corner in Westlands, Nairobi, built around one simple idea: a good cup of coffee can make any day better.

We roast and brew coffee from the highlands of Nyeri and Kiambu, and pair it with fresh pastries baked every morning. Whether you are catching up with friends, meeting a colleague or just taking a quiet moment for yourself, there is always a seat waiting.

Come in, relax, and taste the difference.
